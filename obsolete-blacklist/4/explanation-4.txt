The net has a pentagon with two concave angles. Recall that in order to exclude it via the argument in the 
overleaf we need, in what is called the difficult to case, to show that the edges are no longer than pi. In
fact it is shown in the overleaf that the edges connecting to the concave angles are strictly
shorter than pi. We thus only need to consider the edges 32 and 20. Note however that 32 and 20 are in common with
a convex face which is not a biangle, so they are also strictly shorter than pi.
